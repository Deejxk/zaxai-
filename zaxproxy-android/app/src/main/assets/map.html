One-time setup

Android only lets an app change the GPS after you choose it in your phone's settings. It takes about a minute and you do it once.

  1. Switch on Developer options. Open About phone, find Build number (on some phones it is under Software information) and tap it 7 times.
  2. Choose ZaxProxy Connect. Open Developer options, scroll to Select mock location app and choose ZaxProxy Connect.
  3. Come back here and tap the button again. Your GPS changes straight away.
