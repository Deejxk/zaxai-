Add ZaxProxy GPS to your browser

Do this once in Chrome or Edge. It lets websites see the place you choose on the map.

  1. Open the extensions page. In your browser's address bar type this and press Enter:
    chrome://extensions  (in Edge: edge://extensions)
  2. Switch on Developer mode. It is a switch at the top right of that page (bottom left in Edge).
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder:
    ZaxProxy GPS add-on
  4. Come back here. The line under the button turns green within a minute. Reload any website that was already open.
